Train Times Live

Privacy Policy — Last updated 1 June 2026

Train Times Live does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Data we collect

Nothing sent to our servers

We operate no servers and collect no data. The app communicates only with National Rail's Darwin API to fetch live departure information.

Stored locally on your device

Your saved stations, home/work shortcuts, recent searches, and theme preference are stored only on your device using iOS secure storage. This data never leaves your device.

No analytics or advertising

We use no analytics SDKs, no crash reporting services, no advertising networks, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Third-party services

National Rail Enquiries (Darwin API)

Live departure and arrival data is fetched from Darwin, operated by National Rail Enquiries. Your search queries (station codes) are sent to this API solely to retrieve live train times. National Rail's own privacy policy applies to this data exchange.

Open Government Licence

Train timetable and station data is licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0, supplied by Network Rail and National Rail Enquiries.

Your rights & data deletion

Delete your data at any time

All locally stored data (saved stations, history, preferences) can be cleared from within the app. Uninstalling the app removes all associated data from your device entirely.
